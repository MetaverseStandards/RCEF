This pack does not use restricted FACS manuals, proprietary research, private sessions or internal sources. It reproduces no definitions, illustrations or procedures from FACS manuals. The FACS codes on canonical expression signals are labels: the signal values are not FACS scores. The values of the `facs.code` signals are not FACS scores either, and RCEF does not code faces. The profile names identify public interfaces; `facs.code` names FACS code numbers from a public list; its numeric encoding is RCEF's own and is not a FACS interface. The profile names do not certify anatomy or interoperability.
